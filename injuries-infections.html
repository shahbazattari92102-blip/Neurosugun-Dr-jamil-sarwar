Understanding Head & Spine Injuries

Traumatic injuries to the brain and spine require immediate, expert medical evaluation. Whether resulting from accidents, falls, or impact, these conditions can profoundly affect neurological function and require specialized surgical intervention to prevent permanent damage.

Dr. Muhammad Jamil Sarwar provides meticulous diagnostic assessments and surgical treatments for acute and chronic trauma cases, ensuring patient safety and optimal recovery paths.

Key Conditions Treated

  • Head & Brain Injuries: Concussions, skull fractures, intracranial hematomas (blood clots), and traumatic brain swelling.
  • Back & Spine Injuries: Vertebral fractures, spinal instability, ligament damage, and traumatic spinal cord compression.
  • Spinal Infections & Abscesses: Discitis, epidural abscesses, osteomyelitis of the spine, and deep-seated neurological infections.
  • Related Complications: Post-traumatic neurological deficits, chronic pain syndromes secondary to trauma, and infection management.

When to Seek Immediate Care

Symptoms such as severe persistent headache following trauma, loss of consciousness, weakness in limbs, sudden numbness, loss of bladder/bowel control, or high fever accompanied by severe back pain require urgent medical attention.
