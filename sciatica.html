Understanding Sciatica and Lower Back Pain

Sciatica refers to pain that radiates along the path of the sciatic nerve, which branches from your lower back through your hips and buttocks and down each leg. Typically, sciatica affects only one side of your body. It most often occurs when a herniated disc or bone spur compresses part of the nerve.

Common Symptoms

  • Pain that originates in the lower back and radiates to the buttock and leg.
  • Numbness, tingling, or muscle weakness in the affected leg.
  • Difficulty walking or standing for long periods due to sharp pain.
