Comprehensive evaluation and advanced treatment options for sciatica, nerve compression, and chronic spinal conditions.

Understanding Sciatica

Sciatica refers to pain that radiates along the path of the sciatic nerve, which branches from your lower back through your hips and buttocks and down each leg. It typically occurs when a herniated disc, bone spur, or spinal stenosis compresses part of the nerve.

Dr. Muhammad Jamil Sarwar offers precise diagnostic assessments to identify the root cause of nerve compression, providing targeted treatments ranging from specialized conservative care to advanced spinal decompression surgeries.

Key Aspects of Care

  • Detailed Spinal Assessment: Clinical evaluations combined with advanced spine imaging (MRI/X-rays) to locate exact disc or nerve impingements.
  • Conservative Management: Tailored pain management strategies, physical therapy guidance, and lifestyle modifications for early-stage cases.
  • Minimally Invasive Spine Surgery: Advanced surgical interventions such as microdiscectomy or laminectomy to relieve persistent nerve root pressure.
  • Rehabilitation & Recovery: Structured post-treatment plans to restore spinal strength, flexibility, and mobility.

When to Seek Specialist Evaluation

Severe lower back pain accompanied by shooting leg pain, numbness, "pins and needles" sensation, progressive leg weakness, or any loss of bladder/bowel control requires urgent medical evaluation.
