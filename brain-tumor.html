Brain Tumor Management & Surgery

Precise neurological evaluation and advanced micro-neurosurgical procedures for benign and malignant brain tumors.

Understanding Brain Tumors

A brain tumor is an abnormal growth of cells within the brain or central spinal canal. Tumors can be non-cancerous (benign) or cancerous (malignant), and both types require meticulous evaluation and management due to their impact on delicate surrounding brain structures.

Dr. Muhammad Jamil Sarwar provides specialized diagnostic workups, risk assessments, and sophisticated surgical options aimed at safely removing tumors while preserving vital neurological functions.

Key Aspects of Care

  • Advanced Imaging Evaluation: Comprehensive analysis using high-resolution MRI and CT scans to determine tumor size, type, and exact location.
  • Micro-Neurosurgery: Precision surgical techniques designed to maximize tumor resection while minimizing impact on healthy brain tissue.
  • Symptom Control: Effective management of associated complications such as increased intracranial pressure, headaches, and seizures.
  • Multidisciplinary Coordination: Seamless collaboration with oncology and pathology teams for comprehensive post-surgical care plans.

When to Seek Medical Attention

Persistent, worsening headaches, unexplained nausea or vomiting, vision changes, sudden speech difficulties, progressive weakness, or new-onset seizures require immediate neurosurgical evaluation.
