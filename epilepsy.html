Comprehensive neurological evaluation, precise diagnosis, and advanced medical and surgical management for epilepsy patients.

Understanding Epilepsy

Epilepsy is a chronic neurological disorder characterized by recurrent, unprovoked seizures. These episodes occur due to sudden bursts of electrical activity in the brain, affecting how a person acts, behaves, feels, or temporarily loses consciousness.

Dr. Muhammad Jamil Sarwar offers specialized clinical assessments to pinpoint seizure triggers, evaluate abnormal brain wave patterns, and tailor personalized long-term management plans for patients.

Key Aspects of Epilepsy Care

  • Detailed Diagnosis: Comprehensive history evaluation, neurological examinations, and coordination with advanced imaging (MRI/CT) and EEG diagnostics.
  • Medical Management: Optimization of anti-seizure medications (ASMs) to control episodes effectively with minimal side effects.
  • Drug-Resistant Epilepsy: Specialized evaluations for patients whose seizures do not respond adequately to standard medications.
  • Surgical Evaluation: Advanced neurosurgical considerations and interventions for eligible focal epilepsy cases.

When to Consult a Specialist

If you or a loved one has experienced unexplained seizures, sudden staring spells, repeated involuntary jerking movements, or sudden confusion, professional neurological evaluation is vital to ensure safety and prevent complications.
